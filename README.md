# The Geopolitics of Oil: Panel Evidence on the 2026 Gulf Crisis



Fixed effects panel analysis (Pooled OLS, Fixed Effects, Random Effects) of the effect of the 2026 Gulf Crisis on CPI and industrial production in France, Germany, Italy and Japan (160 monthly observations, Feb 2023 to May 2026), with Arellano HAC robust standard errors. Built in R.

## Files
- Report (PDF): the full written report
- `Panel_Project_Complete.R`: R script producing all results
- `Geopolitics_Oil_Report.tex` and `figures/`: LaTeX source of the report

## Main findings
- The post-crisis period is associated with a modest CPI increase (about +0.96%, p = 0.038)
- The policy rate and the exchange rate are the most significant within-country correlates of CPI
- No statistically significant effect on industrial production (IPI)

## Limitations
Only 4 countries, 3 post-crisis months, and trending series. See Section 8 of the report.

Data: public sources listed in the report's references (IMF, OECD, FRED, BIS)
